SkyWall Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

SkyWall ("the app") is an animated live wallpaper that shows the current time of day and your local weather. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. In short: the app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the developer does not collect or store any of your data.

Information the app uses

Approximate location (optional). If you tap "Use my location" and grant the approximate-location permission, the app reads your device's last known coarse (city-level) location. It never turns on GPS. The coordinates, rounded to about 11 metres or coarser, are sent over an encrypted HTTPS connection to the Open-Meteo weather service to get the weather and sunrise/sunset times for that place. The location is stored only on your device, so the wallpaper can keep working.

City name (optional). If you type a city instead, that name is sent to the Open-Meteo geocoding service to find its coordinates. Those coordinates are then used as described above.

You can use the app without granting the location permission. It will then show a default sky, or the weather for the city you type.

Third-party service

Weather data comes from Open-Meteo, a free weather API that needs no account or API key. Requests contain only coordinates or a city name, plus standard technical information that any web request carries, such as your IP address. Open-Meteo's handling of that information is described in its own privacy policy: open-meteo.com/en/terms.

SkyWall Pro purchase

SkyWall Pro is an optional one-time purchase sold through Google Play. Google Play handles the payment, so the developer never sees your name, email, card or other payment details. To know whether Pro is unlocked, the app asks Google Play on your device whether you own it, and keeps only that yes/no result on your device. How Google handles purchases is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, files or any other personal information.
  • It does not use advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • It does not sell or share data with anyone. Nothing is sent to the developer's servers, because there are none.
  • It does not access your location in the background without the wallpaper being on screen, and never uses precise (GPS) location.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your settings, the last place used and the last weather result in its private storage on your phone. You can remove all of it at any time by clearing the app's storage (Settings › Apps › SkyWall › Storage › Clear storage) or by uninstalling the app. You can also revoke the location permission in Android settings at any time.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone.
